ARQUENTRA Prep

Request account deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your ARQUENTRA Prep account even if you no longer have the app.

Request by email

  1. Email an account deletion request from the Google email address connected to ARQUENTRA Prep.
  2. State that you want your ARQUENTRA Prep account deleted. Include any different email address you used for exam notifications only if you also want those entries removed.
  3. Follow the instructions in our reply to verify control of the account address and any separate waitlist address.

If the email link does not open, send your request to arquentraprep@gmail.com with the subject “ARQUENTRA Prep account deletion request”. If you cannot access the original email address, contact us for verification options. Never send passwords, verification codes, or identity documents in your initial request.

Delete in the Android app

Open Profile, choose Delete account, and verify the same Google account when prompted.

What deletion covers

Deletion covers your ARQUENTRA Prep account, associated cloud study progress, and waitlist entries under the verified account email. On the device where you delete in the app, it also clears saved account data and signs you out. If you request deletion from the web, clear local app storage on your devices if you also want the local copies removed.

Retention

After we verify your identity and confirm the request, we delete your account and associated student data from the live service immediately. ARQUENTRA keeps no separate student-data backups. Supabase may retain provider-managed backups and operational logs under its plan and recovery settings. Deletion from the live service does not immediately erase those copies.

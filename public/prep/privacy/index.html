ARQUENTRA Prep

Privacy policy

Arjun Babu (ARQUENTRA) operates ARQUENTRA Prep. For privacy questions or requests, contact arquentraprep@gmail.com.

Guest practice and device data

You can study without a cloud account. The app saves study activity, results, learning and revision progress, and preferences on your device. Offline downloads and display preferences are stored locally. Doubt searches are saved on the device for reuse.

Google sign-in and cloud synchronization

If you choose Google sign-in, the app uses your Google name, email address, and identity token to connect your account. Google provides the sign-in service. Supabase verifies identity, manages sessions, and stores account-linked cloud study data, including test answers and results, study activity, flashcard reviews, lesson progress, and saved Full Mock attempts. The app uses that data to restore and synchronize your progress.

Exam launch notifications

If you request a notification, ARQUENTRA Prep sends your email address, selected exam, and name if provided to the Supabase waitlist. It uses those details to track your request and contact you about the selected exam. The app also saves request status on your device.

Retention and deletion

After we verify your identity and confirm the request, we delete your account and associated student data from the live service immediately. ARQUENTRA keeps no separate student-data backups. Supabase may retain provider-managed backups and operational logs under its plan and recovery settings. Deletion from the live service does not immediately erase those copies.

Signing out preserves the cloud backup. You can request account and associated data deletion in Android Profile or through the public account deletion page. You can also contact us about a waitlist signup made with a separate address. The app clears local account data when you complete deletion in the app. Web requests handle server data, so you can clear app storage separately on any device you still use.

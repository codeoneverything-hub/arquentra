# Changelog

## Website refresh — 2026-11-02

- Focused the message on freelance website design and development for growing businesses; simplified the primary CTA and made WhatsApp the clearest contact path.
- Replaced the heavy hero video with a lightweight CSS website preview.
- Added service scope, packages, process, FAQs, privacy information and transparent concept case studies with interactive demos.
- Added three practical guides, metadata, sitemap, structured data and social share artwork. City pages are withheld until unique location details exist.
- Added an enquiry email endpoint with validation and a WhatsApp/email fallback; email delivery is inactive until Vercel credentials are configured.
- Kept testimonials, owner details, policy promises, prices and timelines hidden until verified details are supplied.
- Added optional Plausible analytics; no tracking runs unless configured.
